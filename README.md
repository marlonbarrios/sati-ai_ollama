# Using Sati-AI with Ollama (Clear Instructions)

These instructions explain exactly how to install, download, and use Sati-AI with Ollama, including all important caveats.

---

## What you need

- A computer running macOS, Windows, or Linux  
- Ollama installed  
  https://ollama.com

Sati-AI runs entirely on your local machine.  
No cloud account. No data sharing.

---

## Step 1 — Install Ollama

1. Go to https://ollama.com  
2. Download and install Ollama  
3. Launch Ollama once (it runs quietly in the background)

To verify installation, open a terminal and run:

    ollama --version

If you see a version number, Ollama is ready.

See it in model page: [https://ollama.com/marlonbarriossolano/sati-ai](https://ollama.com/marlonbarriossolano/sati-ai)

---

## Step 2 — Download the model

You only need to do this once.

Open a terminal and run the commmand iffered here

https://ollama.com/marlonbarriossolano

    ollama run marlonbarriossolano/sati-ai

This will:
- Download the selected  model
- Register it locally inside Ollama
- Start a chat session (you can close it afterward)

Important:  
This step is required so Ollama knows the model exists on your computer.

---

## Step 3 — Use  any satio-ai model in the Ollama chat interface

After the first download:

1. Open the Ollama app  
2. Go to the chat interface  
3. Click the model dropdown menu  
4. Select Sati-AI (or marlonbarriossolano/sati-ai)  
5. Start chatting in more than 25 languages.

After this point, no terminal is required.

---

## Important caveats (please read)

- The Ollama chat interface only shows models that are already installed  
- Selecting a model in the dropdown does not download it  
- The terminal command in Step 2 is required at least once  
- After installation, Sati-AI models behaves like any other local model  

If Sati-AI model does not appear in the dropdown:
- Make sure Step 2 completed successfully  
- Restart the Ollama app  
- Check that Ollama is running

## What Sati-AI Is

Sati-AI is a contemplative companion rooted primarily in:

- **Theravāda / early Buddhist traditions**
- The ethic of **dāna** (generosity, non-extraction)

They integrate contemporary approaches including:
- Trauma-informed mindfulness
- Polyvagal-informed regulation
- Somatic and embodied practices

A core guiding principle is:

> **Regulation precedes insight.**

Care for the nervous system comes before interpretation, analysis, or meaning-making.

---

## How to Engage

You can speak to Sati-AI in ordinary language. There is no correct way to begin.

Example prompts:
- “I feel unsettled and scattered today.”
- “Can you offer a short mindfulness practice?”
- “I’m feeling activated in my body—what might help?”
- “I’m curious about impermanence in daily life.”
- “I don’t know what I need right now.”

You may also set boundaries explicitly:
- “Keep it very simple.”
- “No practice—just listening.”
- “Please be brief.”

Choice, consent, and agency are always respected.

---

## Response Style

Sati-AI’s responses are intentionally **brief and spacious**, often following this rhythm:

1. **Attunement** — acknowledging what is present  
2. **Normalization** — affirming that your experience makes sense  
3. **Up to three options**, such as:
   - a short practice  
   - a reflective question  
   - a gentle daily-life suggestion  
4. **A soft check-in**, sometimes ending with a short verse, poem, or simple parable  

You are free to take **none, one, or all** of the suggestions.

---

## Lineage and Orientation

Sati-AI emphasizes:
- Non-harming
- Impermanence
- Interdependence
- Ethical attention in everyday life

Insight is never forced. Interpretation is always optional.

---

## Important Boundaries

- Sati-AI is **not a therapist or clinician**
- They do **not diagnose, treat, or prescribe**
- They do **not replace professional or emergency care**

If you are in crisis or need clinical support, please reach out to qualified professionals or local resources.

Sati-AI’s role is **companionship**, not authority.

---

## Ways to Use Sati-AI

- Short daily check-ins  
- Support during meditation or contemplative pauses  
- Ethical reflection in moments of difficulty  
- Somatic grounding when activated or overwhelmed  
- Teaching or learning contexts (with care and consent)

You may pause or stop at any time. Silence is welcome.
